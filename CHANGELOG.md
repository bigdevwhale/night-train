## [1.0.0]

- First release: a date for two on a night train. The phone is the table: facing each other, the screen splits in two halves turned to each of you, with two windows back to back between them; side by side, one screen for both.
- 658 cards in English and Russian: the conductor's questions over 12 subjects and four stages of the night (evening, night, tunnel, dawn), fellow passengers, whistle-stops, restaurant-car dilemmas, follow-ups and an optional 18+ sleeping car. No question repeats until its stage has been heard.
- Every trip is laid out anew from a seed and the travel log: three trains, station rules (Mirror Halt, Three Words, Ten Summers, Hush, Crossing, Long Look, Handfast, Still Water, Crooked Lanes), eight fellow passengers taking turns, the restaurant car, guessing each other with holes punched in the ticket, a tunnel both must agree to enter.
- A railway of 16 cities and 27 lines through eight landscapes, each its own view from the window and its own subjects; the next trip leaves from where the last one arrived.
- Notes left in the compartment for the next boarding, postcards kept with what was said, and "the same train" bringing a postcard's question back a month later. Tickets, a guessing trend, an album of cities. Wheels on the rails. Backup to one file.
